Watch in VR & AR

Ride the film's camera inside every scene, with the soundtrack and narration.

What you need

A VR headset with a WebXR browser: Meta Quest 2 / 3 / 3S / Pro (Meta Quest Browser), Pico 4, Apple Vision Pro (Safari, visionOS 2), or a PC headset with Chrome or Edge.

How to start

  1. Put the headset on and open its web browser.
  2. Go to
  3. Wait for the film to load, then choose Watch in VR and allow the immersive view.
  4. Want the slow-motion flythrough without narration? Start Experience mode first, then press VR in the player bar.

Controls

  • Trigger · A / X play / pause
  • Thumbstick ← → skip 5 seconds
  • Grip leave VR
  • Pinch play / pause on Vision Pro

Sit or stand still: the camera glides for you and cuts fade through black. Chapter headings float ahead of you.

Put each scene on your table as a small model in a glass case, and walk around it while the film plays.

What you need

An Android phone with Chrome and Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), or a Meta Quest 3 / 3S with passthrough.

How to start

  1. Scan a code above (or open #arlite in Chrome, or the Quest browser).
  2. Pick a chapter if asked, wait a few seconds for it to load, turn the sound on, then choose View in AR and allow the camera.
  3. Move the phone slowly over a table until the gold outline turns bright: that is the surface found, and the outline is the model's size.
  4. Tap to place the film. It locks to the table; tap again to glide it somewhere else.
  5. Pinch to make it bigger or smaller, twist two fingers to turn it, and walk around it to see the full depth from any side. Small (the default) keeps a model on the table and the camera view clear; Full shows the whole scene. Use the on-screen play / pause button. To leave AR, tap Exit (top right or in the bottom bar) or use your phone's Back gesture.

On Quest 3

  • Trigger place / move the model
  • A / X play / pause
  • Grip leave AR

Works best on a plain, well-lit table with some texture (wood, cloth), not glass or a glossy white top.

iPhone and iPad: Safari does not support WebXR yet, so VR and AR cannot start there. Watch the film or Experience mode instead. Embedded previews (such as the claude.ai page) block VR and AR, so open the film's own web address.
